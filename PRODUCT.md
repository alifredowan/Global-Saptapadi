## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Educated Hindu diaspora professionals (ages 26–38) residing in the UK, USA, Canada, UAE, Singapore, India, Bangladesh, and Nepal seeking lifelong, intentional marriage.
Secondary: Their parents, elders, and families who participate directly or in coordination to review biodata, check family compatibility, and consult family pandits.

## Product Purpose

Global Saptapadi exists to provide a sacred, dignified, and humanized matrimonial home for Hindu couples and families worldwide. Success means moving beyond superficial dating app mechanics and sterile SaaS databases into meaningful, reverent matchmaking rooted in the seven sacred vows of the Saptapadi, leading to lifelong unions and family blessing.

## Positioning

Unlike swipe-driven dating apps or generic, noisy commercial matrimonial portals cluttered with ads and spam, Global Saptapadi treats matrimony as a sacred family milestone. It offers verified identity checks, privacy-first photo control, structured 36-Guna Kundali horoscope matching, dedicated Pandit consultations, and dual-seat family collaboration where both the individual and their family are honored.

## Operating Context

- Used in thoughtful domestic spaces: living rooms with parents, evening conversations across time zones between overseas candidates and parents back home.
- Intertwined with cultural rituals: biodata exchanges, horoscope matching (Kundali/Patrika), family introductions, Pandit consultations, and wedding milestone celebrations (Shubho Bibaho).
- Emotional stakes: high vulnerability, cultural pride, desire for authenticity, and absolute intolerance for casual dating gimmicks or generic "AI slop".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Laravel 11/12 application with Tailwind CSS 4, Blade templates, Alpine.js / vanilla JS.
- Dedicated dashboards for Brides, Grooms, Families, and Pandits.
- Horoscope matching engine (36 Gunas Vedic Ashtakoota system).
- Privacy controls: photo blur/private unlock upon mutual acceptance.
- Realtime messaging and proposal management.
- Heritage-driven, non-generic presentation: avoid sterile cards, cookie-cutter pill badges, and generic corporate UI.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Global Saptapadi
- Core Ritual: The Saptapadi — Seven Sacred Steps around the holy Agni fire.
- Visual World: Handcrafted Heritage & Heirloom Invitation. Warm handmade paper textures, kumkum maroon (#8B1E2D), sacred agni saffron (#E07B1A), haldi gold, and delicate organic Alpona/mandala flourishes.
- Voice: Dignified, warm, reverent, family-honoring, and authentic. Never transactional or frivolous.

## Product Principles

1. **Sacred, Not Transactional:** Every interaction reflects the gravitas of a lifelong sacrament, not a marketplace transaction.
2. **Family Harmony & Individual Autonomy:** Both the candidate and their family have a dignified voice without encroaching on personal boundaries.
3. **Organic Warmth over Sterile Tech:** Warm paper grounds, artisanal typography, and bespoke traditional touches replace generic cold SaaS patterns.
4. **Uncompromising Discretion & Trust:** Privacy, verified identity, and mutual consent are foundational guarantees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AAA contrast standard on body text and essential interactive controls.
- Responsive across mobile smartphones used by parents and high-resolution desktop displays used by diaspora professionals.
- Clear legible typography with generous line-height and scalable font sizes.
